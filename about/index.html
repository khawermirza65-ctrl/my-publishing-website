About InkWell Publishings

We are a publishing-services business. Authors come to us to prepare, design, publish and market books they own.

What we do and do not do

We offer editing, cover design, formatting, publishing-platform assistance, distribution guidance and marketing support. We do not offer traditional publishing contracts, and we do not take ownership of your book.

How we work

Each project begins with a conversation and a written scope. You review work at each stage, and your publishing accounts stay in your name.
