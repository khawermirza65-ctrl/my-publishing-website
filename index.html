Author control

Your book stays yours.

You keep your rights and the accounts where your book is sold. Platform fees, print costs and retailer deductions still apply; we explain them rather than hide them. Scope and fees for each project are put in writing.

Questions

Before you enquire.

Do I keep my rights?

Yes. InkWell provides services; it is not a traditional publisher and does not acquire your book.

Can you guarantee sales?

No. We help prepare and publish your book properly; outcomes depend on many factors outside our control.
