A CLOSER LOOK

Turn a written book into a considered listening experience

Clear decisions, practical deliverables and room for your input.

01

Prepare the text for listening

A manuscript written for the page may include tables, captions, footnotes or links that need a spoken treatment. Decide how those elements will be handled before recording. Confirm the approved text and chapter order so the audio can be checked against one final version.

02

Support for authors who narrate

If you plan to read your own book, start with a short sample. It can reveal room noise, recording consistency and pacing issues before an entire manuscript is recorded. Agree the recording setup and practical guidance before starting. A quiet, repeatable environment is more useful than trying to repair every problem afterwards.

03

Editing and mastering have different roles

Audio editing removes agreed mistakes and improves continuity. Mastering prepares the recording to the destination’s technical requirements. Neither automatically fixes every recording problem. Review a sample first and define whether pickup recordings, chapter separation and quality checks are included in the project.

04

Keep the release requirements visible

Cover specifications, file naming, chapter structure and technical checks depend on the intended platform. Account access and release choices should remain with the author. InkWell’s support is a scoped service; it does not retain a royalty share under that model. Platform and distribution charges may still apply.

Questions about this service

Can I narrate my own audiobook?

Yes. A sample recording helps assess the setup and identify issues early. Self-narration guidance, editing and mastering can be scoped around your material.

Does the service include a professional narrator?

Do not assume narrator casting is included. Narration arrangements and any additional costs need to be agreed before recording begins.

Can you fix noisy audio?

Some problems can be improved, but severe noise, clipping or inconsistent recording may require a new take. A sample assessment comes first.

Can I narrate my own book?

Often yes; talk to us about equipment and quality requirements.
