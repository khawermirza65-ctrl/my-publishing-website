Audiobook support

Support for planning, preparing and publishing an audiobook edition.

Ask about this service

Who it is for

Authors considering an audio edition, including those who want to narrate themselves.

The problem it solves

Audio has its own technical standards and platform requirements.

What is included

  • Planning the audiobook edition
  • Guidance on narration options and file requirements
  • Help preparing for platform submission

What we need from you

  • Final manuscript
  • Narration preference
  • Any pronunciation notes

How it works

  1. Discussion
  2. Plan narration
  3. Review audio
  4. Submission support

Scope boundaries: Exact deliverables are agreed in writing. Platform acceptance is decided by the platform.

Questions about this service

Can I narrate my own book?

Often yes; talk to us about equipment and quality requirements.
