Children's book illustration

Illustration support for picture books, from character design to finished spreads.

Ask about this service

Who it is for

Authors of picture books who need consistent illustrated characters and scenes.

The problem it solves

Picture books depend on illustration that stays consistent across pages.

What is included

  • Character and style discussion
  • Illustration of agreed scenes
  • Preparation of artwork for layout

What we need from you

  • Manuscript and page plan
  • Descriptions or references for characters
  • Chosen trim size

How it works

  1. Brief
  2. Character concepts
  3. Scene illustrations
  4. Files for layout

Scope boundaries: Scope, spread count and revisions are agreed in writing before work starts.

Questions about this service

Do I own the illustrations?

Usage rights should be written into your agreement.
