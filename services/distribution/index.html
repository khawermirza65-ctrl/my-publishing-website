A CLOSER LOOK

Understand where your book can be available

Clear decisions, practical deliverables and room for your input.

01

Availability and placement are different

Distribution can make a book discoverable or orderable through supported channels. It does not mean a shop will stock it on a shelf, a library will buy it, or a retailer will actively promote it. Start with the markets and formats you want, then choose a route that fits those goals.

02

Match the route to the format

A print-on-demand book, an ebook and an audiobook can follow different distribution paths. We help organise the relevant files and book information for the agreed platforms. Existing accounts, previous editions and exclusivity choices should be reviewed first to avoid duplicate listings or conflicting arrangements.

03

Get the book details consistent

Title, author name, description, ISBN information, categories and pricing need to agree across the release. For print distribution, discuss the implications of retailer discounts and returns before making selections. These decisions can affect your proceeds and costs; they should not be treated as small technical settings.

04

Plan for updates and maintenance

A later correction may need a new file upload or metadata update on each platform. Agree who will maintain those accounts after launch. Platform charges, printing costs and retailer terms are separate from our service fee. Your book rights remain yours, while proceeds depend on the channels and settings you choose.

Who it is for

Authors who want readers to be able to order their book beyond a single store.

The problem it solves

Distribution terms differ by platform, and being listed is not the same as being stocked.

What is included

  • Explanation of distribution routes for print and ebook
  • Metadata and ISBN guidance
  • File preparation to platform requirements
  • Help setting up accounts in your name

What we need from you

  • Final files and cover
  • Decision on which retailers and formats to pursue
  • Your ISBN details, if you hold one

How it works

  1. Choose formats and channels
  2. Prepare metadata
  3. Submit files
  4. Check live listings

Scope boundaries: Distribution makes a book orderable; it does not guarantee shelf placement, library purchases or sales. Retailer fees and deductions apply.

Questions about this service

Does distribution put my book in every bookstore?

No. An orderable listing is different from physical stocking. Each retailer or library makes its own purchasing decisions.

Can you help with an already published book?

Share the current platforms, ISBN details and account ownership. We first need to check the existing setup and any restrictions.

Do I keep my royalties?

InkWell does not acquire your book rights through a service project. Net proceeds still depend on printing, platform and retailer charges and your chosen settings.

Does distribution put my book in bookshops?

It can make it orderable by shops. Whether any shop stocks it is the shop's decision.
