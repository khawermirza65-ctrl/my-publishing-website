Book editing and proofreading

Manuscript editing, copyediting and proofreading for independent authors, with changes you review and approve.

Ask about this service

Who it is for

Authors with a finished draft who want a trained second reader before the book goes to layout.

The problem it solves

Typos, inconsistent names, unclear passages and uneven pacing are hard to see in your own work.

What is included

  • Manuscript review to recommend the right level of edit
  • Copyediting for grammar, consistency and clarity
  • Proofreading of the laid-out pages
  • Tracked changes and comments you accept or reject

What we need from you

  • Your complete manuscript in Word or a similar format
  • Any style preferences, spellings and character or place names
  • Your target readers and publishing plans

How it works

  1. Discussion and manuscript sample
  2. Agreed edit level in writing
  3. Edit, then author review
  4. Proofread after formatting

Scope boundaries: Editing improves the text; it does not guarantee reviews, sales or rankings. Fact-checking and legal review are outside standard editing.

Questions about this service

What is the difference between copyediting and proofreading?

Copyediting works on language, consistency and flow. Proofreading is a final check for slips once pages are laid out.

Will my voice change?

Suggestions are tracked, so you decide what stays.
