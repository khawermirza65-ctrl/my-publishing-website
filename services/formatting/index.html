Book formatting and typesetting

Print layout and EPUB formatting so your interior reads cleanly on paper and on screen.

Ask about this service

A CLOSER LOOK

An interior that feels effortless to read

Clear decisions, practical deliverables and room for your input.

01

Print and ebook need different files

Print formatting sets a fixed page with controlled margins, typography and page breaks. A reflowable ebook adapts to the reader’s device and font settings. A print PDF is therefore not a substitute for a well-prepared EPUB. Choose the formats you want before production so each receives its own checks.

02

Design the reading experience

A good interior gives chapter openings, headings and ordinary paragraphs a consistent rhythm. We plan the treatment of front matter, contents pages, running heads, page numbers and back matter. Tables, footnotes and image-heavy books need discussion early because they can affect both layout complexity and ebook behaviour.

03

Prepare the manuscript for production

Send the approved manuscript with clearly marked headings and images supplied separately at suitable quality. Include acknowledgements, dedication, copyright information and any author biography you want to use. Avoid changing the main text during layout; repeated revisions can shift pagination and create avoidable corrections.

04

Check the book in its final format

Review the print PDF at the intended trim size and inspect an ebook preview on more than one screen size. Look at contents links, image placement and chapter navigation. Platform previews and a physical proof are important final steps. Delivery should identify the approved files and any changes made after your review.

Who it is for

Authors with an edited manuscript who need print-ready and ebook files.

The problem it solves

Word files rarely export into clean books; margins, headings and page numbers need deliberate setting.

  • Reflowable EPUB for ebook retailers
  • Proof review before final files

What we need from you

  • Front and back matter text

Scope boundaries: Image-heavy or highly designed interiors are scoped separately. Formatting changes after final approval may need a new scope.

Questions about this service

Can you format a book with photographs or tables?

Yes, the complexity and number of visual elements need to be reviewed before quoting. Provide the images, captions and a sample table with your manuscript.

What if I change the manuscript after formatting?

Small corrections may fit within the agreed review round. Substantial additions or rewrites can require repagination and a revised scope.

Do you choose my trim size?

We can help compare suitable options for the genre and distribution route. You approve the choice before the interior and cover are finalised.

Is print formatting the same as ebook formatting?

No. Print uses fixed pages; ebooks reflow to the reader's screen, so each is built differently.

Can you work from a Word file?

Yes. A manuscript using consistent styles makes the process faster.
