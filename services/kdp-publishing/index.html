Amazon KDP publishing assistance

Help with setting up your own Kindle Direct Publishing account, book details and file uploads.

Ask about this service

A CLOSER LOOK

Prepare an Amazon KDP release with fewer loose ends

Clear decisions, practical deliverables and room for your input.

01

Keep the account under your control

Publishing assistance should support your own Amazon KDP account. Your identity, tax information and payment details belong to you. We help organise the release and explain the steps, while account verification and platform decisions remain with Amazon. Do not send passwords or sensitive financial documents through an enquiry form.

02

Bring the production files together

Before upload, the manuscript and cover must match the formats you intend to release. Check the author name, title, subtitle, edition details and any ISBN information for consistency. The print cover should use the correct template, and ebook navigation should work before the files are submitted.

03

Give readers an accurate listing

Book descriptions should explain the subject or story and who it is for. Keywords and categories should describe the actual book rather than unrelated popular searches. Metadata planning is part of making a clear listing, but it cannot guarantee placement, sales or bestseller status.

04

Review before you publish

Use the available preview tools to identify file issues, then review pricing, territories and distribution selections. Decide carefully before joining any programme with exclusivity conditions. For print, order and inspect a proof when possible. Submission is followed by the platform’s review; its acceptance and timing are outside our control.

Who it is for

First-time authors who want to publish on Amazon but find account and listing setup confusing.

The problem it solves

KDP asks for details on rights, categories, pricing and files, and mistakes can delay a launch.

What is included

  • Walkthrough of KDP account setup under your name
  • Help preparing title, description, categories and keywords
  • Upload and previewer checks for your files
  • Explanation of pricing and royalty options

What we need from you

  • Final print and ebook files
  • Your own tax and payment details, entered by you
  • Book description and author bio

How it works

  1. Account and rights check
  2. Listing preparation
  3. Upload and preview
  4. Your final review and submission

Scope boundaries: You own your KDP account and rights. Amazon's fees, print costs and royalty rules still apply. InkWell is not an Amazon partner and cannot influence approval or ranking.

Questions about this service

Will the book be published in my name?

The author name and publishing details are agreed with you. Your KDP account stays under your control.

Can you guarantee Amazon approval?

No. We can help prepare files and address issues, but Amazon reviews submissions and controls its platform.

Can I sell an ebook elsewhere too?

That depends on your programme choices and their current terms. Review any digital exclusivity requirements before enrolling or distributing elsewhere.

Do I keep control of my account?

Yes. The account, rights and payments stay with you.

Will this make my book sell?

No service can promise sales. We help the book go live correctly.
