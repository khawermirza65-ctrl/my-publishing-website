Book marketing

Book launch planning and practical marketing materials for independent authors.

Ask about this service

A CLOSER LOOK

Build a launch around real readers

Clear decisions, practical deliverables and room for your input.

01

Begin with the reader and the book

Book marketing works better when it starts with a clear audience rather than a list of promotional channels. Tell us what the book offers, who it is intended for and what you have already built. A launch for a debut novelist may need a different approach from a nonfiction book supported by an existing newsletter.

02

Prepare the foundation before promotion

An accurate book description, clear cover, functioning sales links and useful author biography give promotional activity somewhere to lead. We can scope work on listing copy, author positioning and launch materials. A coherent message across your website and social channels helps readers understand why the book is relevant to them.

03

Choose a manageable set of activities

Depending on the brief, support can include a launch plan, social content, newsletter copy or press-release preparation and outreach. Agree the deliverables, channels and schedule before work begins. Paid advertising or third-party placement costs should be identified separately; they are not silently included in a service description.

04

Measure attention and follow-through

Define which outcomes can be observed, such as clicks, enquiries, email sign-ups or attributed purchases. A campaign report should separate work completed from results achieved. Media coverage, reader reviews, rankings and sales cannot be promised. For authors without an advertising budget, start with a realistic organic plan and build steadily.

Who it is for

Authors preparing a launch who want a plan instead of scattered posting.

The problem it solves

A good book still needs a clear description, a reader profile and a few reliable channels.

What is included

  • Reader and positioning discussion
  • Launch plan and checklist
  • Book description and author bio copy
  • Starter email and social content plan

What we need from you

  • Your book summary and goals
  • Existing audience, mailing list or social accounts, if any
  • Launch date, if set

How it works

  1. Discovery discussion
  2. Plan
  3. Materials
  4. Review

Scope boundaries: We prepare plans and materials. We do not promise sales, bestseller status or reviews, and we do not buy reviews.

Questions about this service

Can I market my book without paying for ads?

An author website, useful content, newsletters and appropriate community participation can support organic discovery. They still require consistent work and time.

Will you make my book a bestseller?

No marketing service can responsibly guarantee bestseller status, reviews or a specific number of sales.

What should I share before requesting a plan?

Send your genre, release date, sales links if live, target readers, current audience channels and available budget. That helps avoid an unsuitable proposal.

Do you guarantee a bestseller?

No, and nobody honestly can.
